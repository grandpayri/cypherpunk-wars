DEPOSIT_TO_MY_VAULT funds your own gameplay: a plain send from your wallet into your covenant-locked Gameplay Vault. Anyone can pay INTO the vault (receiving is unrestricted) -- only spends OUT of it are covenant-enforced to CPW transactions.
