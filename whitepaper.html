VERSION 1.0 // THE_DECENTRALIZED_MANIFESTO
ABSTRACT: This document outlines the transition of the legacy "Archmage" systems into a fully decentralized, DAG-native warfare protocol. By leveraging the speed and security of Kaspa, we enable an autonomous game state that exists entirely within the BlockDAG, removing the need for central authority or fragile web servers.
01. The Vision

Cypherpunk Wars is more than a game; it is an experiment in digital permanence. Every action is tagged onto a real Kaspa transaction's native payload field, so the game's state is reconstructible by any client that scans the chain, as long as the network itself survives. Kaspa's own roadmap points toward a future architecture called vProgs (Verifiable Programs) for richer on-chain execution -- we see this bunker's tagged-payload-and-covenant approach as a working proof of concept for that direction, not a claim that vProgs already exist.

02. Proof of Energy

In this ecosystem, every action is a real Kaspa transaction, drawn from a covenant-locked Gameplay Vault. This ensures that every Phish, every Attack, and every Sector expansion has real-world weight -- measured in Turns, not an invented resource. A Turn is real network fuel (KAS) spent, not a fictional currency layered on top of it.

03. The Social Contract

The protocol is designed to be self-sustaining. Through a community-driven "Cistern" or manual Peer-to-Peer fuel transfers, the barrier to entry is lowered by the success of the established veteran operators. As one bunker grows, it provides the exhaust (energy) needed to ignite the next.
